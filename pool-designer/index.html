        Do I need to install any software?+

        No. Poolly runs in a modern web browser with no software to install.

        Can I save my design and come back to it?+

        Your project workflow can save and share the design as you move toward builder quotation.

        Does the designer work on mobile?+

        Yes. Poolly works on mobile, although a larger screen gives you more room for detailed 3D editing.

        Can my pool builder use my design?+

        Yes. Your design can be shared as a clear visual and quote-ready project brief for professional review.
