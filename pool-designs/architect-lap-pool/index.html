Architect's Lap Pool

12-metre lap lane between concrete walls

A pure swimming machine. The Architect's Lap Pool maintains a uniform 1.3 m depth across a 12-metre lane, flanked by board-formed concrete walls. Graphite glass tile deepens the water to a near-black mirror, reflecting sky and architecture in equal measure.
