Raised Geometric Spa

Overflowing raised spa, bronze detailing

A destination in itself. The Raised Geometric Spa lifts the water plane above the deck, spilling over a crisp weir edge into a lower catch. Bronze-tone glass mosaic and bronze-anodised detailing introduce warmth against board-formed concrete — a quiet dialogue between metal, stone, and water.
