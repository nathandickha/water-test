Courtyard Plunge

Compact square for tight urban sites

Proof that a pool need not be large to be extraordinary. The Courtyard Plunge fits a 3-metre square into the tightest urban footprint, with a raised honed-bluestone edge that doubles as seating. Deep blue glass tile gives the small volume surprising depth and presence.
