Modern Rectangular

Clean lines, raised spa, silver travertine

A study in restraint. The Modern Rectangular pairs a precise 8-metre basin with a raised, connected spa framed in silver travertine. Pacific Blue glass mosaic at the waterline catches low evening light, while French-pattern limestone paving extends the living space outward — blurring the line between architecture and water.
