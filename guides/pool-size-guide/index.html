Design guide

Pool size guide.

Choose dimensions by starting with how the pool will be used, then balancing the pool against the wider outdoor space.

Start with use, not a standard size

A pool for regular lap swimming requires a different proportion from a compact plunge pool or a family entertaining pool. List the activities that matter most before setting dimensions.

Preserve the space around the pool

The pool should not consume every available metre. Allow for compliant barriers, movement, furniture, planting, access and the relationship to the home.

Account for steps and benches

Entry features improve usability but reduce clear swimming area. Model them early so the internal dimensions remain practical.

Test the concept in 3D

Use the designer to compare alternative proportions. A small adjustment to width, step position or spa placement can significantly change the balance of the design.

Important

Dimensions, barriers, setbacks, structural requirements and safety rules vary. Confirm the final proposal with qualified local professionals and relevant authorities.
