Pool Planning

This guide explores pool planning in depth — the practical considerations, the design options, and how each decision affects the final pool. Use it alongside the 3D studio, where you can test these choices in real time.

Understanding pool planning

Every element of a pool contributes to how it performs, how it looks and how much it costs to build and run. pool planning is no exception. The choices you make here interact with the pool's shape, its finishes, its features and its site, so it pays to understand the options before committing.

In the 3D studio you can adjust pool planning and immediately see the effect on the whole design. This makes it far easier to weigh trade-offs than reading specifications on paper ever can.

There is no single right answer when it comes to most pool decisions — only the answer that best suits your site, your budget and the way you intend to use the pool. Consider how each option performs across the full life of the pool, not just on the day it is handed over.

No pool element exists in isolation. A choice in one area often constrains or enables choices elsewhere. When you adjust the design in 3D, take the time to see how each change ripples through the whole.
